02 WHO WE ARE

More than a building.
A better space.

Gumbi Construction is a construction company based at 5 Fatsia Pl, Katlehong, 1488, providing general construction services across South Africa. With over five years of industry experience, we help property owners turn ideas into practical, well-finished spaces.

Our work covers new building projects, renovations, concrete, tiling, roofing, ceilings, plumbing, electrical work, painting and ongoing property maintenance. Whether the project is a single repair or a larger improvement, our aim is to deliver dependable service and quality workmanship.
